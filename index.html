A & Jay Daily Backups

This private administration tool copies the rental application's database and vehicle evidence files to a designated Google Drive backup folder for recovery.

It is operated by A & Jay Car Rentals for its own business records. It is not a public Google Drive service and does not offer customer sign-in.

Access and storage

Only the business owner authorizes the Google account used for backups. Backup files remain private; no inspection reports, customer details or photographs are published on this website.

For backup access, recovery or privacy questions, contact aandjayvehicleinspection@gmail.com.
