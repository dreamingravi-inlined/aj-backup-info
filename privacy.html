Privacy policy

A & Jay Daily Backups is an internal backup tool operated by A & Jay Car Rentals.

What the tool accesses

The tool uses Google Drive authorization to create, read and verify backup files in a designated backup folder. Its Google Drive permission can technically access the authorizing account's Drive; the backup configuration directs its work to the backup folder. It does not intentionally collect unrelated Drive files.

What backups contain

Backups may include the rental database, customer and rental details, vehicle inspections, photographs, signatures, staff records and related business records already held by the rental application.

Purpose and recipients

Data is copied to the business's designated Google Drive account for disaster recovery and backup verification. Authorized backup operators and the hosting providers used to run the backup process may process the data for that purpose. Backup data is not sold, used for advertising or published on this site.

Retention and control

Backup archives are retained indefinitely unless the business owner arranges deletion. Deleting a record from the live app does not automatically remove it from older backups. The owner can revoke the tool's Google access through Google Account settings; this stops future access but does not delete existing backup files.

Security and contact

Backups are intended to remain private, with access limited to authorized operators. Credentials must be kept out of public repositories and pages. For access, correction, deletion or security questions, contact aandjayvehicleinspection@gmail.com.
